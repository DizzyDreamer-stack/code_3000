Collection of Assignments for CSE 3000: Contemporary Issues in Computer Science and Engineering

AI is a fairly new frontier in many fields, like healthcare and retail. It makes our lives much more easier. As a student, I definitely can attest to the fact that AI is woven into the fabric of my academic career. Personally, I feel that technology moves a lot faster than the current legal system the US has in place. This means a lot of things slip through the cracks that shouldn't in an ideal society. For instance, there are AI tools that can use the picture of a person to generate an image or video that shows the person nude, simply from a picture. A notable person who this has happened to is Taylor Swift in 2024. While there have been bills and statements put forward by US lawmakers, but with the way new updates keep coming out, it seems like the law can't keep up.

While we're on the topic of ethics, AI privacy issues aren't the only wrinkle. AI itself isn't inherently supposed to be bad. However, it is the way models and classifiers are built that can affect whether the impact of the AI tool in question is negative or positive. For instance, a lot of AI tools have been shown to be biased against marginalized communities like black people. Many AI companies preach fairness and equity but the data that AI models have based their work on can be biased. These biases can leak through and affect fields like education and healthcare, which have rippling effects.

A value that matters a lot to me is authenticity. It may seem pretty cliche, but growing up, in school, I definitely felt like I never really knew the true feelings of the people around me. It definitely messed with my social development a lot seeing as I turned to books and academics to retreat in a safe space where I kind of had a modicum of control. It sounds like I'm drifting off, but until I got to college and I met a lot of my current friends, I always waited for another shoe to drop subconsciously, and I feel that authenticity is my way to truly discern safety in any form.
